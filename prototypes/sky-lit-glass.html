Sky-lit glass. The sky's color washes into each panel and the weather plays on the glass: raindrops gather, dry and run down; snow drifts past with frost at the corners; the sun glints across and lights floating dust; mist drifts in fog; lightning flickers through every panel. Each effect is drawn once into a small tile when the weather changes and after that only moves or fades, so the GPU does it without redrawing; it all pauses when the window is hidden.
