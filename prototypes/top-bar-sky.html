How it's drawn. Clouds are generated once per weather change from layered noise, shaded from above so they have lit tops and darker undersides, and drawn at half resolution (clouds are soft, so nobody can tell). After that they only slide sideways at two depths, which the GPU does without redrawing. Rain and snow are three depth layers of pre-drawn streaks and flakes that only slide down. Lightning lights up the clouds themselves, with a new randomly branched bolt each strike. The moon shows tonight's real phase. Recoloring for the time of day reuses the stored cloud shapes, so no noise is recomputed. Nothing runs per frame, and everything pauses when the window is hidden.
